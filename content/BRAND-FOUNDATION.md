# BRAND-FOUNDATION.md

## Who Soudyn Labs is

An independent software studio founded and led by Sahal (founder, product architect, designer and primary developer), based in Kerala, India. Websites are the first revenue line and the way the studio learns how businesses work. The long-term goal is a set of focused, industry-specific software products. TravelOS, a management platform for travel agencies, is first.

## Positioning

**Promise:** Software that takes repetitive work off your team.

**Statement:** For small and mid-sized businesses, Soudyn Labs designs and builds websites and business software that save time, because we start from the recurring problem, not from a feature list.

**What the site must make a visitor feel within 10 seconds:** these people are a product company with taste, they are small enough to care, and they can ship.

## Message pillars

1. **Problems first.** We start with the task that costs you the most time.
2. **Simple on purpose.** Fewer features, clearer workflows, little training.
3. **Built with care.** Design, speed, accessibility and reliability are part of the job.
4. **Long-term partner.** We stay after launch and improve what people actually use.

## Voice

Calm, plain, specific. A capable person explaining their work without selling it.

- Sentence case everywhere, including headings and buttons.
- Plain verbs. Say what happens: "Start a project", "Join early access", "Send message".
- Name things by what the reader understands, not how they are built.
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Active voice by default.
- Errors say what happened and how to fix it. They do not apologize.

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| "We build the simplest version that solves it." | "We leverage cutting-edge synergies." |
| "Quotes retyped. Payments chased by hand." | "Streamline your workflows holistically." |
| "In development. Join early access." | "Revolutionary AI-powered platform." |
| "Tell us what is eating your team's time." | "Unlock your business potential today!" |

### AI language

AI works quietly in the background inside the products. Mention it as a practical tool ("smart reminders", "reads documents so you do not retype them"), never as a headline or a badge. Do not use the words revolutionary, next-gen, game-changing or powered by AI in headlines.

### Proof policy

Only publish what is true and checkable. No invented numbers, testimonials, logos or awards. Work shown must be approved by the client (see `CONTENT-CHECKLIST.md`).

## Logo

Source file: `soudyn-logo-transparent.webp` (612 x 408, transparent).

What it is:
- A layered, angular S-shaped mark on the left, then the wordmark in a geometric sans.
- "Soudyn" in white, "Labs" in silver (about #AFAFAF).
- Artwork occupies only the central area of the canvas. Crop to the artwork plus padding before use (artwork box is roughly 390 x 87 px inside the file, ratio about 4.5 to 1).
- It is white on transparent, so it only works on dark backgrounds.

Usage rules:
- Use only on Lab Navy (`#0F1F3D`) or Raised Navy (`#162B52`). The site header and footer are always navy so the white logo is always legible.
- Minimum width 120 px on screen. Mark alone minimum 24 px.
- Clear space on all sides: half the height of the mark.
- Do not recolor, outline, add effects, stretch or place on photos.
- Mark alone (the angular S) is used for favicon, app icon and the OG image corner.
