## TRAVELOS

**Status:** In development
**h1:** TravelOS: run your travel agency from one workspace.
**Lead:** One place for customers, documents, visas, quotations and payments, with reminders that go out on time. Built with travel agencies, in development now.
**Button:** Join early access
**Mock caption:** Concept preview

### The problem
- h2: Travel agencies run on scattered details.
- Rows:
  - **Passports and documents arrive in chats.** Someone has to find, rename and file them.
  - **Visa status lives in a spreadsheet.** Or in one person's head.
  - **Quotes and invoices are typed again.** Every booking starts from zero.
  - **Payment follow-ups are manual.** Reminders depend on memory.

### What it will do
- h2: What TravelOS will do
- Customers and documents: Keep every customer record and document in one place and see what is missing at a glance.
- Visas and passports: Track each application step and get reminded before anything expires.
- Quotations and invoices: Create them from saved details instead of retyping.
- Payments and reminders: See who has paid, who has not, and send reminders from the same screen.
- Team and reports: Assign tasks, see what is pending and review the month without a spreadsheet.

### Principles
- h2: How we are building it
- **Quiet automation.** Software reads documents and drafts reminders so your team does not retype.
- **Few clicks.** Each task should take the fewest steps possible.
- **Built with agencies.** We design it around how agencies actually work. Early access members shape what comes next.

## ABOUT

**h1:** We build software that stays out of the way.
**Lead:** Soudyn Labs is an independent software studio in Kerala, India. We make websites for businesses today and focused software products for specific industries next.

### Story
Soudyn Labs started with websites, because building them taught us how businesses really run: the same tasks, done by hand, again and again. That is where our products come from.

We do not start with "what should we build?" We start with "what recurring problem costs a business time or money?" Only then do we design, and we design the simplest thing that works.

### Principles (rows)
- **Problems first.** We start from a recurring problem, not a feature list.
- **Simplicity over complexity.** Clean, few clicks, little training.
- **Quality over quantity.** Fewer things, finished well: design, speed, accessibility, reliability.
- **Long-term thinking.** We would rather build something that lasts than something quick.
- **Keep improving.** Nothing is ever finished. Customer feedback shapes what we change.

### How we use AI
- h2: How we use AI
- Body: We use AI to build faster and to remove busywork inside our products, such as reading documents or drafting reminders. It works in the background. People still make the decisions, and we review everything we ship.
